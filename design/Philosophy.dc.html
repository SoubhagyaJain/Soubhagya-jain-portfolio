05/Engineering philosophy
“What breaks when this leaves localhost?”

A clean demo hides its failure cases. I spend most of my time on the ones that surface later — the answer that sounds right, the tool that half-worked, the context that quietly went stale, the run that passed yesterday and failed today.

The system as the demo shows it

That is the layer of engineering I care about most — where a prototype either becomes a system you can trust, or falls apart under reality.
