01/RAG + AI Reliability

Aperture RAG

A local-first retrieval system built around one constraint: if the evidence is weak, the answer should not pretend otherwise.

Hybrid retrieval, reranking, multimodal evidence, verification and observability work together to produce grounded answers.

Engineering note

One orchestration method grew to 1,365 lines and controlled routing, retrieval, reranking, generation and verification.

I refactored it into isolated pipeline stages while preserving behavior through tests.
