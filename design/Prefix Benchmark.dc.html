Shared-Prefix LLM Inference Benchmark

Thirty sequential requests to a local qwen2.5:7b on Ollama, with ~2,800-token prompts that either share a prefix or don't. When the prefix repeats, its KV state is reused: prefill nearly disappears, time to first token falls with it — and decode costs what it always did.
