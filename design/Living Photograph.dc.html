01/Intro

I build AI systems that have to work beyond the demo.

What interests me starts after the prototype works. Retrieval quality and grounding. Agents that verify instead of guessing. Evaluation that catches regressions before users do. Inference cost, latency and the trade-offs between them. Observability, so a bad answer leaves a trace you can follow. Most of the engineering lives in those places not in the first working call.
